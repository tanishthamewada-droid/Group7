About Before You Buy

Before You Buy helps people understand district-level land-use information before purchasing land. It brings current LULC statistics into one simple dashboard, then turns important follow-up checks into a practical checklist.

See the area

Explore district-level spatial context and approximate centers.

Understand the data

Compare agricultural, built-up, forest, and water coverage from the API.

Know what to verify

Follow up on ownership, encumbrances, conversion, and planning.

Important: This app does not verify ownership, certify legal status, establish plot boundaries, or decide whether land is safe to buy.
