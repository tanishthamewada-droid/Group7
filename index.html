About Before You Buy

Before You Buy helps people understand district-level land-use information before purchasing land. It brings Bhuvan LULC statistics and map layers into one simple dashboard, then turns the important follow-up checks into a practical checklist.

See the area

Explore the selected district with an OpenStreetMap base and Bhuvan land-use overlay.

Understand the data

Compare agricultural, built-up, forest, and water coverage reported by the API.

Know what to verify

Use the checklist to follow up on ownership, encumbrances, conversion, and planning.

Important: This app provides area-level information. It does not verify ownership, certify legal status, establish plot boundaries, or decide whether land is safe to buy.

For a responsible purchase decision, compare these insights with current official records and seek qualified legal or local professional advice.
