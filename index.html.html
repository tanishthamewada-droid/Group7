About Before You Buy

This page displays district-level land-use land-cover statistics returned by the Bhuvan LULC service.

Select one of the supported districts and the 2005–06 or 2011–12 survey cycle to view the reported total area and positive land-use classes in square kilometres.

These statistics do not provide plot boundaries, ownership, legal status, zoning, permissions, or property verification. The Bhuvan response is limited to the district name, survey cycle, total area, and land-use breakdown exposed by this application.

Data is requested through the backend using its configured Bhuvan token. If the upstream service does not return usable data, the backend uses its configured district fallback values.
